# Sharing and downloading

Share or download the report from the **Inspection report** bar on the report page. It has three icons: **share**, **email** and **download**.

## Download a PDF

1. Open the report.
2. Click the **download** icon on the Inspection report bar.
3. The PDF downloads to your computer.

The PDF contains the full report: vehicle details, images, damage annotations, assessment table, tyre information, checklist responses and (if enabled) repair estimate.

## Share the web report by email

Send a link to the web version of the report directly to the customer.

1. Open the report.
2. Click the **share** icon on the Inspection report bar.
3. Enter the recipient's email address.
4. Send the link.

The customer receives an email with a link to view their report online. The link is read-only.

{% hint style="info" %}
The share link gives the recipient read-only access to the report. They cannot edit damages or download the PDF unless you send the PDF separately.
{% endhint %}

## Share a PDF by email

To send the PDF directly as an email attachment:

1. Open the report.
2. Click the **email** icon on the Inspection report bar.
3. Enter the recipient's email address.
4. Send the PDF.

{% hint style="info" %}
The exact sharing options available may vary depending on your retailer setup.
{% endhint %}
