# Troubleshooting and FAQ

Common questions about the ClearInspect Retailer app.

## Signing in

**I cannot sign in.**
Enter the User ID and password your administrator registered for you, then tap **Login**. If sign-in fails, re-enter them carefully and check your details are correct. If it still fails, contact your administrator.

**I forgot my password.**
Tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen and enter the email address registered with your account. A password reset link is sent to that email.

## Capturing images

**An image will not capture.**
Improve the lighting, give yourself space, and clean the lens. If it still will not auto-capture, turn on the Manual toggle (top right) and tap the shutter. If there is no Manual toggle and your overlay stays white, your setup captures manually - tap the shutter for every view.

## Vehicle details

**The vehicle details are wrong.**
Edit the make, model or bodystyle on the vehicle input screen. Any change is logged.

## Connection

**The app is offline. Will I lose my work?**
No. Inspections work offline and sync when you reconnect. Keep the app open and do not delete it before the data uploads.
