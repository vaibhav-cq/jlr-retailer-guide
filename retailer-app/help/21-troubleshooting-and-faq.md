# Troubleshooting and FAQ

Common questions about the ClearInspect Retailer app.

## Signing in

**I cannot sign in.** Enter the User ID and password your administrator registered for you, then tap **Login**. If sign-in fails, re-enter them carefully and check your details are correct. If it still fails, contact your administrator.

**I forgot my password.** Tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen and enter the email address registered with your account. A password reset link is sent to that email.

## Capturing images

**An image will not capture.** Improve the lighting, give yourself space, and clean the lens. If it still will not auto-capture, turn on the Manual toggle (top right) and tap the shutter. If there is no Manual toggle and your overlay stays white, your setup captures manually - tap the shutter for every view.

## Vehicle details

**The vehicle details are wrong.** Edit the make, model or bodystyle on the vehicle input screen. Any change is logged.

## Connection

**The app is offline. Will I lose my work?** No. Inspections work offline and sync when you reconnect. Keep the app open and do not delete it before the data uploads.

## App crashed or closed

**The app closed unexpectedly during an inspection.** Reopen the app and go to the home screen. Your inspection appears as an **In progress** card - tap it to carry on from where you left off. Completed steps are saved on the device, and anything not yet uploaded syncs once you are back online, so keep the app installed and do not delete it. When you resume, check the last view you were capturing and, if it did not save, recapture it following the overlay.
